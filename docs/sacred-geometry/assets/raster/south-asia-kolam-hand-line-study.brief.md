## What this image must communicate

A **kolam** (Tamil Nadu) is a threshold drawing made by looping a single continuous line around a dot grid. The line itself is the subject: it creates rhythm, closure, and spatial field through its pressure and continuity. This image must demonstrate that quality — not just the finished shape.

## Drawing method to depict

- Graphite or dry media on off-white paper, or digital equivalent with visible pressure
- Show the **dot grid** (pullis) lightly placed first — small dots in a regular grid
- Draw the looping line over the dots, showing how the line bends, thickens at turns, and lifts at junctions
- Include **at least one in-progress state**: the dots visible with partial looping, then the completed shape
- Line weight variation: thicker at curves, lighter on straight segments

## Compositional structure

- Format: landscape, approximately 1200×800px minimum
- Three states shown left to right:
  1. Dot grid only (pullis, 5×5 or 7×7)
  2. Loop partially drawn — about 40% complete
  3. Completed kolam loop, filling all dots

## Linework quality requirements (expressive-linework-raster criteria)

- Pressure variation clearly visible — no flat vector-like uniform strokes
- Slight tremor or human variation at line edges is desirable
- Dots remain visible through the completed loop (transparent treatment)
- Overall rhythm: the eye should track the line continuously around the field

## Cultural context notes

- Kolam is a living daily practice, not a museum artifact — the image should feel contemporary and active
- Do NOT use sacred imagery (deities, temple symbols) as the subject for the loop pattern
- A simple square-spiral or floral loop on a 5×5 dot grid is the right starting point
- Source reference: Tamil Nadu kolam tradition; Marappachi Kolam (simple border loops for beginner learners)

## Rights and production

- Original drawing only — do not copy or trace any published kolam photograph
- Artist should sign off on the dot-grid count and loop correctness before final export
- Export as WebP, minimum 80% quality, no lossy artifacts on line edges
