## Purpose

This source board collects reference citations, rights notes, and search-query seeds for all `reference-board` assets associated with the Islamic eight-point star. Before any museum image is used in an ArtisanalCanvas card, it must appear here with rights status confirmed.

---

## What to look for

The `reference-board` asset type for this concept should show:
- Museum objects where the 8-pointed star appears as a primary geometric element
- Architectural contexts (tilework, carved stucco, wooden screens)
- Portable objects (metalwork, manuscript illumination, ceramic tiles)
- Construction diagrams or historical geometry manuscripts (if any survive)

Do NOT use:
- Images protected by copyright without permission
- Sacred or devotional objects without a context card
- Images that mix the 8-star with other iconography without noting the distinction

## Confirmed reference sources

### Alhambra Palace, Granada, Spain
- **Museum:** Patronato de la Alhambra y Generalife (Spanish state)
- **Object type:** Architectural tilework, stucco panels, geometric floors
- **Relevant sections:** Patio de los Arrayanes, Hall of the Ambassadors, Mexuar

## Historical geometry manuscripts

The classic medieval Islamic geometry source is:

**Anonymous, "On Similar and Complementary Interlocking Figures" (Fi Tashbih al-Ashkal)**
- Compiler: Abu al-Wafa' al-Buzjani (940–998 CE), *On the Geometric Constructions Necessary for the Artisan*
- Contains construction diagrams for regular polygons and star forms
- Modern translation: Alpay Özdural, "Mathematics and Arts: Connections between Theory and Practice in the Medieval Islamic World," *Historia Mathematica* 27(2), 2000
- **Status:** Academic reference only. Do not reproduce manuscript folios without library permission.

**Thabit ibn Qurra** and **Al-Farabī** wrote on geometric constructions but surviving illustrated MSS are rare.
