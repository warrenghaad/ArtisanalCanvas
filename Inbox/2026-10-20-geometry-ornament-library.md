## Routing

GitHub is the research collection's source destination. The corresponding graphics and catalog metadata are intended for the existing Geo + Arts = Innovation Supabase visual-assets table. Figma can consume the transparent PNG layers; Notion and Google Drive can consume the research document; Replit can use the repository files. Those consumption paths are prepared by these portable assets, not evidence of automatic synchronization or completed writes to those services.

The research collection is separate from the Academy application's runtime. The current app's Neon/Drizzle connection does not automatically display these Supabase research records.
