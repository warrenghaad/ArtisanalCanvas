You are Codex working inside my Project EUCLID / Geo-Arts Curriculum repository.

Your task is to create a backend-ready geometric ontology and historical nesting system for:

1. GEA = Geometric Element Atomic
   - Irreducible geometric primitives.
   - Includes basic geometry primitives and the 32 Petzinger universal Ice Age signs / insignia.
   - Examples: point, line, angle, arc, circle, oval, crescent, triangle, square, rectangle, chevron, cross, zigzag, spiral, dot cluster, ladder, tectiform, penniform, claviform, etc.

2. GEM = Geometric Element Molecular
   - Any recurring compound made from two or more GEAs.
   - GEM may be:
     - GEA + GEA
     - GEA + GEM
     - GEM + GEM
   - Examples: rosette, 8-point star, mandala, tessellation, lattice, grid, meander, stepped pyramid profile, sun disk with rays, winged disk, cylinder seal border system.

3. GECD = Geometric Element × Carrier × Dimension
   - A historically documentable node where a geometric element or molecular compound appears on or inside a material, ritual, architectural, technical, or institutional carrier.
   - Example:
     - Element: circle
     - Carrier: Shamash tablet / sun disk / cylinder seal / potter’s wheel
     - Dimension: 2D relief, 3D vessel, rotational mechanism, architectural plan
   - GECD nodes are the historical anchors of the system.

4. GEK = Geometric Element Knowledge / Concept
   - Functional or mathematical truths revealed through accumulated use of GEAs/GEMs.
   - Examples: radius, diameter, rotational symmetry, reflection symmetry, tessellation, proportion, extrusion, nesting, tiling, modular repetition.

5. GEpHR = Geometric Element Presentation / Metaphor / Human Register
   - Meaning-bearing uses of geometry.
   - Examples: circle as divine justice, crescent as lunar change, rosette as fertility or sacred bloom, triangle as stability, square as order, rectangle as record-keeping, symmetry as balance.

The goal is to instantiate a full registry and inference pipeline that can track how geometric elements begin as primitives, combine into molecular forms, nest into artifacts, become functional inventions, and accumulate into historical geometric intelligence over time.

Important project constraints:
- Do not rename canonical EUCLID vocabulary.
- Preserve the distinction between Day A metaphor-register and Day B function-register.
- Do not collapse metaphor into function or function into metaphor.
- Treat the same geometric form as readable in multiple registers.
- Time is the exogenous variable.
- Geometry is the autoregressive accumulated substrate.
- GECD nodes are historical evidence anchors.
- Artifacts and inventions should never be recreated if a record already exists; update, merge, or extend existing records instead.

Build the following system.
